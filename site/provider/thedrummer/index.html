What to know before you commit

TheDrummer publishes 3 models in the tracked catalogue. Prices below are per million tokens as published in the provider's own pricing metadata and refreshed daily. Two practical traps: long-context requests are often billed at a higher tier above 128K–200K tokens, and cached input is billed separately — a cheap headline rate with no cache tier can end up costing more than a pricier model with large cache discounts.
