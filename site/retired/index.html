Why models disappear

Providers delist endpoints for three common reasons: a dated snapshot is superseded by a newer one, a preview model is withdrawn, or a free tier is closed after an abuse wave. If you depended on a retired model, the cheapest replacements are ranked on the cheapest API pages, and every removal is dated in the daily change log.
