Privacy policy

This site is a static website. It has no accounts, no login, and no user-submitted content. The only personal data it can receive is what you type into the optional newsletter form, plus standard server logs and advertising or analytics cookies described below.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is stored by the newsletter provider named on the form and used only to send daily price-change emails. Unsubscribe links appear in every email.

Advertising

Third-party ad vendors (for example Google AdSense) may set cookies or use device identifiers to serve and measure ads. Where required by law, a consent message is shown before non-essential cookies are set. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google Ads settings.

Analytics

If aggregate analytics are enabled they are configured without cross-site tracking or personal identifiers.

Affiliate links

Outbound provider links may be affiliate links and are marked with rel="sponsored". Following one may set a cookie from the provider.

Data we publish

The only dataset published here is model pricing metadata, which contains no personal data.
