About TokenPrice Watch

A one-page answer to a question every engineering team asks: what do LLM APIs actually cost today?

What this is

TokenPrice Watch rebuilds the entire public LLM price catalogue every single day and keeps the diff. It exists because published price lists drift: providers add snapshots weekly, cut rates without announcement, introduce cache tiers, and retire models. A spreadsheet copied last quarter is reliably wrong.

What this is not

It is not a benchmark site, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any model provider. Benchmark indices shown here are the values published alongside the models in the public catalogue; they are quoted, not measured by us. Prices are list prices in USD and exclude negotiated discounts, minimum commitments and failed-request retries.

How it makes money

Advertising and affiliate links to inference providers. When a provider link earns a referral, that is disclosed on the page with a sponsored link attribute, and it never changes a ranking: every table on this site is sorted by price or by a published benchmark index.

Corrections

If a price here is wrong, it is almost always because the upstream catalogue changed and the next daily run will fix it. To flag something urgent, email peiqing6888@gmail.com.
